The problem

Clients call to book today, and two people sometimes get the same time. I want them to pick a free slot from their phone.

Who will use it

Salon customers on their phones, and the owner on a small laptop.

Acceptance criteria

Your application answers each one. Delivery is checked against the same list.

  1. 1A customer can book a 30-minute slot from a phone without creating an account.
  2. 2The salon receives a message for every new booking within one minute.
  3. 3Two customers cannot book the same slot, even at the same moment.
  4. 4The owner can close days and hours from one simple page.
  5. 5The site works in Arabic and English on phones and computers.
